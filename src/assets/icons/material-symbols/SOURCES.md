# Google Material Symbols

Source: https://fonts.google.com/icons

Official repository: https://github.com/google/material-design-icons

Pinned revision: `10ecc14790612ca8fd164e3f4ffe9c16fc50d5b7`.

Style: Material Symbols Outlined, 24 px. The SVG files are unmodified copies of `symbols/web/<name>/materialsymbolsoutlined/<name>_24px.svg` at that revision. Imported as Astro SVG components; `Icon.astro` sets their size and `currentColor`, hides decorative icons from assistive technology and prevents keyboard focus. No icon font, CDN requests, client-side library or added dependency is used.

Included symbols: `add`, `arrow_right_alt`, `arrow_upward`, `close`, `content_cut`, `location_on`, `menu`, `north_east`, `remove`.
